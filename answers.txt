A:
Pro 1: Easier to manage a big project by allowing to break down and keep track of smaller tasks.
Pro 2: Good way to divide a project between a team and have everybody on the same page about progress of the project
Con 1: Can take a bit of time to set up a big project
Con 2: Not using agile can make it easier to just get started with the development process

B:
The scrum master is the person who manages the standup meetings every day and keeps a track of the jira development board
The product owner is the person who uses the software and interacts with the actual solution, they are not involved in the development process

C:
Sprint Planning: Define the sprint goal, and Decide how tasks will be allocated to team
Sprint Review: Showcase completed tasks, and gather feedback from team and shareholders
Spring Retrospective: Discuss what went well in the project, Figure out potential code improvements
